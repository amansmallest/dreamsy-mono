## 3-minute demo script
1. **Say it (0:00).** On the phone: Create → "a cozy farming game where crops sing at night" → Create · 100K coins. The timeline starts with the kit template, sprites and playtests.
2. **Already built (0:30).** Switch to a game built earlier today (Crownbreak Chess or Skyforge) and play it for 20 s. Point out the tutorial, controls and art.
3. **Click a sprite (1:00).** On dreamsy.dev or the app, tap a sprite → "make the shield red". Only that sprite changes, in about 30–60 s.
4. **Play and get paid (1:15).** Maharshi plays (or remixes) one of your games on his phone. Your phone buzzes **"You got paid · +9K coins"** (+25K for a remix).
5. **Multiplayer (1:45).** Quick match in Gem Drop Duel or Orb Duel on two phones: red vs blue, screens in sync.
6. **Leaderboard (2:15).** Skyforge 1-1 results card → Race the #1 ghost.
7. **Close (2:40).** "Say a game. Play it today. Creators keep 90%."
