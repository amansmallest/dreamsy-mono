# Sharing on Dreamsy

How games and players spread, and what is live on prod today.

## Share a game
- **Share button** on every game page in the app. The native share sheet sends "Play *Game* on Dreamsy" with a link.
- **QR code:** the app's scanner opens games and group invites.
- **Opens in the Dreamsy app.** Friends get the game page, with Play (10K coins, or free for your own games) and Remix.

## Family & friends groups
- **Create a group:** Profile → *Family & friends* → *New group*. It gets an invite link `https://dreamsy.dev/join/<code>`, shared through the share sheet.
- **Join:** enter the 8-character code in the app, scan the QR, or open the link.
- **Share a game with the group:** use *Share with family* on any game. Unpublished drafts work too, and members play drafts free.
- **Group leaderboards:** each game has a private board for your group next to the public one.
- **"New family member"** notifications when someone joins.
- **Limits:** up to 50 members. The owner can make a new code (the old one stops working), leave, or delete the group.

## Remix (spin off)
- Any published game can be remixed into your own copy, which you then edit by prompt or by tapping a sprite.
- Remixing costs **25K coins, all of it paid to the original creator**. Every play of your spin-off also pays them a **1K royalty**. See PRICING.md.
- Remix lineage shows on the game page, so the original stays credited.

## Leaderboards & ghosts
- Racing, platformer, runner and puzzle games time every run in game time (pauses don't count).
- The results card shows your rank, personal best, the top 5, **Race the #1 ghost** and Play again.
- Boards are **Public** and per **group**. Ghosts are see-through replays of the best run that you race head to head.

## Multiplayer invites
- Inside a multiplayer game: **Create room** gives a 6-character code. A friend taps **Join**, types the code, then Ready, then Start.
- **Quick match 1v1** pairs two players automatically, red vs blue. See MULTIPLAYER.md.

## Feed & discovery
- Home has **Trending**, **New** and **Following**, plus a **Multiplayer** shelf (filtered with `?multiplayer=1`).
- Every game card shows a **Multiplayer · up to N** badge and its play price.
- Search and Discover have a Multiplayer filter.

## "You got paid" loop
Play, remix or buy in someone's game, and the creator's phone buzzes "You got paid". It also lands in their inbox (bell) and Earnings card. That's the hook that turns sharing into creator income.
