# Lanternlight, chapter 1: arrival, the dark lamp, the boathouse (point-and-click).
@chapter ch1 "The Dark Lamp"
@label start
@music tide
@bg harbor fade
> The ferry noses through the fog toward Saltreach. The lighthouse stands black against the night sky.
> Its lamp, which should sweep the water every twelve seconds, is out.
@show ott neutral right
Ott: Mind the gap, keeper. This island doesn't forgive wet boots.
Ott: Forty years I've rowed this crossing. Never once seen the light dark.
* "Thanks for bringing me out in this, Ott." -> ch1_thanks {aff ott 1}
* "Why is the light out?" -> ch1_why
* Say nothing and step onto the jetty. -> ch1_jetty {aff ott -1}

@label ch1_thanks
Ott: Hah. Manners. You'll not last a winter with those.
Ott (sad): But thank you. Not many say it.
@goto ch1_jetty

@label ch1_why
Ott (angry): How should I know? I'm a ferryman, not a lamp-polisher.
Ott: Ask the girl. She's been up there all night, cursing at the brass.
@goto ch1_jetty

@label ch1_jetty
@sfx door
> The jetty boards are slick. Somewhere above, a door bangs open and a lantern bobs down the cliff path.
@show mira worried left
Mira: You're the relief keeper? Oh, thank the tides.
Mira: I'm Mira. Apprentice. Second year. The lamp died at midnight and I cannot get it to take.
Mira (worried): The supply boat is due before dawn. If it can't see the Teeth, it'll find them the hard way.
* "Show me the lamp. We'll fix it together." -> ch1_together {aff mira 1}
* "You've been at it all night? Go and rest; I've got this." -> ch1_together {aff mira 1} {aff ott 1}
* "An apprentice who can't light a lamp. Wonderful." -> ch1_together {aff mira -2}

@label ch1_together
@if aff mira >= 1
  Mira (happy): Right. Yes. This way. Mind the seventh step, it wobbles.
@else
  Mira: ...This way. The seventh step wobbles. Try not to fall down it.
@endif
@hide ott
> Ott mutters something about tides and turns his boat for the far cottage.

@label ch1_lamproom
@bg lamproom fade
@show mira worried left
> The lamp room smells of brass polish and paraffin. The great lens throws your lantern back at you a hundred times.
Mira: Look at the burner. The wick is gone. Not burned down. Gone.
> You lean in. The stub of the old wick ends in a clean, straight edge.
@clue cut_wick
Mira: Someone cut it. Who would cut a lighthouse wick?
* "Someone who wanted the light out. Do you keep spares?" -> ch1_spares
* "Maybe you trimmed it too short and won't admit it." -> ch1_accuse {aff mira -1}

@label ch1_accuse
Mira (worried): I've trimmed that wick every night for two years. I know what a burned wick looks like.
Mira: That is not a burned wick.
@goto ch1_spares

@label ch1_spares
Mira: Spare wicks are in the boathouse toolbox. But the key went missing weeks ago and Ott never bothered to cut a new one.
Mira: And a dry wick won't light in this damp. It needs oil first.
@if aff mira >= 1
  Mira (happy): You go. I'll clean the lens. Shout if anything bites.
@else
  Mira: I'll stay with the lens. Try not to drop anything in the harbour.
@endif
@hide all
@autosave
@music mystery
@explore boathouse

@label ch1_out
@sfx door
@bg lamproom fade
@music tide
@show mira worried left
Mira: You found one? And oiled! Quick, give it here.
@take oiled_wick
> Mira threads the wick with fast, sure fingers. You strike the match.
@sfx lamp
@flash #fff4c0
> The flame catches, climbs, and the lens drinks it up. A beam of white light walks out across the water.
@set lamp_lit
Mira (happy): It's turning. It's turning!
@if aff mira >= 1
  Mira (happy): I could kiss you. I won't. But I could.
  @aff mira 1
@else
  Mira: ...Thank you. Really.
@endif
> Far out on the water, a small light blinks twice in answer and swings away from the Teeth. The supply boat is safe.
> But somebody on this island put the light out on purpose. And there are only three of you here.
@goto ch2_start
