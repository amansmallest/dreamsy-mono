> You find Ott on the jetty, staring at the Teeth. You don't have to say much. He's been waiting for someone to ask.
Ott (sad): Aye. It was me. I rowed across at midnight, cut the wick, rowed back.
Ott: There's a skiff, the Gannet. Smugglers. They use our light to find the cove. I thought one dark night and they'd run onto the Teeth and leave us be.
Mira (worried): The supply boat was out there too, Ott! Those are our friends!
Ott (sad): I know. I knew it by one in the morning. I couldn't row back fast enough to undo it.
* "Then we stop the Gannet properly. Together. Report them to the harbour board." -> ch2_together {aff ott 2} {aff mira 2}
