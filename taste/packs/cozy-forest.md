Near-monochrome green world with very soft contrast; navigation is shaped by rounded bush walls.
- Walls are thick rounded hedges with a lighter inner rim and leafy scalloped edges; floors are light grass with small clover/leaf specks and sandy clearings.
- Accents are rare and small: orange fruit, mushrooms, yellow flowers — they mark pickups and points of interest.
- The player is tiny, pale (cream/white) with a thin dark outline so it's always visible on green.
- Round trees: big soft canopy with 2–3 green tones and a darker underside.
- Low contrast overall; readability comes from shape, not color.
