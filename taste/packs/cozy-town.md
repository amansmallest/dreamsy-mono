Lived-in top-down town (3/4 view): muted, slightly desaturated, warm-grey world with lots of small readable props and signage.
- Muted greens for nature, warm greys and beige for concrete, one warm accent (mustard yellow, brick red) for points of interest.
- Outlines: 1px darkest purple-grey #41354b on objects, not on ground tiles.
- Dense prop language: vending machines, signs, fences, rails, crossing lights, posters, crates. Props tell stories.
- Pine trees as stacked layered triangles with a light top-left edge; grass with tiny tufts.
- Light from top-left; cast shadows as a darker tone of the ground, offset down-right.
