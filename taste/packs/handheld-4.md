Classic handheld look: EXACTLY four colors, darkest to lightest. Nothing else, ever (backgrounds, UI, text, sprites).
- 8×8 tile grid; sprites 8×8 or 16×16. Bold, iconic, high-readability shapes.
- Background is the darkest tone; platforms and walls use the mid tones with lightest-tone top edges; hero and pickups use the lightest tone so they pop.
- Levels are named rooms that fit one screen each (THE PIT, FOREST, TEMPLE...), with a tiny HUD strip at the bottom: hearts and a coin counter.
- Decoration through repeated motifs: brick patterns, vines, skulls, torches, moon. Dithering allowed only as checkerboards of two adjacent tones.
- Chunky pixel font, all caps.
