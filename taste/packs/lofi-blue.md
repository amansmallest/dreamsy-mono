Melancholic, cinematic two-tone: the world is near-monochrome charcoal, and ONE accent (sky blue) carries all the light and emotion.
- Everything structural (machines, walls, debris, cables) in 4–5 charcoal greys with crisp light-grey edge highlights; heavy detail, clutter, cables.
- Only light sources, screens, windows, the sky and important objects use the blue accent ramp. Light spills onto the floor as hard-edged blue shapes.
- The hero is a small silhouette that reads by contrast against the blue.
- No other hues. If something must stand out (danger, pickups), make it the lightest blue/white.
- Quiet, lonely atmosphere; slow pacing; minimal HUD in light grey.
