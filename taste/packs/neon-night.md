Top-down retro night city strip (Miami/Vegas vibe): purple asphalt, warm orange streetlight pools, neon signs.
- Base world is cool and dark: violet-blue roads with pale lavender lane markings, crosswalks and X-junctions; dark purple sidewalks with a subtle crosshatch texture.
- Lighting is the art: soft round pools of warm orange under every street lamp, overlapping on sidewalks and roads; long dark cast shadows from palms and buildings toward the bottom-right.
- Neon signs glow in hot pink #ff4fa0, cyan #4fe0ff and yellow, with a soft halo of the same color around them.
- Buildings as flat rooftops with parapets, AC units, skylights; lit windows in warm yellow. Palm trees seen from above (star of green fronds over a trunk).
- Cars: small top-down sedans and taxis in muted saturated colors, catching orange light.
- Mood: warm humid night, cinematic, a bit dangerous.
