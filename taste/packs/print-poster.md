Swiss/modernist print poster made playable: cream paper, ink-black only, halftone dots for every grey. Best for puzzle, strategy and board-like games.
- Two inks: cream paper #fdf5df and near-black #111111. NO other colors. Tones come from halftone dot patterns (SVG <pattern> of black dots on cream, or cream dots on black) at 3–4 densities (≈20/40/60/80%), never flat grey.
- Strict grid: square cells separated by thin cream gutters; tiles are flat halftone squares of different densities that encode terrain/ownership.
- Pieces are bold iconic silhouettes (towers, houses, flags, gates), solid black on light cells and solid cream on dark cells — instantly readable, no detail inside.
- Paths/moves drawn as thin black lines with rounded right-angle turns and small arrowheads.
- Typography is the hero: huge tight bold grotesk title (Helvetica-like, tight leading) top-left, tiny italic serif credits below; small square icon buttons top-right (outlined or solid black squares with one symbol).
- Generous empty paper margins, a little paper speckle/noise; asymmetric, calm, confident layout. Animations are minimal and snappy (slide, flip, stamp).
