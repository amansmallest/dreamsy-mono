Nostalgic summer countryside, quiet and wide. Blue is the main color: skies, water, shadows, distant hills all lean blue; greens are cool and teal-shifted.
- Shadows hue-shift toward blue/indigo, never toward black. No pure black anywhere; darkest is deep indigo #2a3170.
- Big painterly cumulus clouds built from 3–4 flat tones with jagged pixel edges. Layered parallax: far hills (pale blue), fields (blue/green patchwork), near foliage (teal), foreground crops.
- Characters are small (16–24 px tall) and simple: 1px dark-indigo outline, warm skin/hair accents so they pop against the cool world.
- Foliage: clusters of small leaf shapes with a light top edge; repeat patterns with slight variation.
- Calm mood: generous sky, horizon in the upper third, little UI.
